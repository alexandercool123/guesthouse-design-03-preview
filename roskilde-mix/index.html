Roskilde B&B is a modern country home/retreat with a panoramic view - quietly situated, yet just a 2 minutes' drive from a brandnew well-stocked supermarket. In our guesthouse you are 10-12 minutes’ drive from lakes, beaches, shops and restaurants in Roskilde and Solrød and you can view historical sites from your bedroom. Business travellers may like to know that Greve, Lille Skensved, Hedehusene and Ølby with various distributional and industrial plants are of similar distance. Copenhagen airport is located 31 minutes away.

We offer free fastspeed Wi-Fi, as well as bright rooms and complimentary tea/coffee. Your own 4G mobile connection will also work here.

Roskilde B&B tilbyder overnatning i moderne faciliteter og med fri vestvendt udsigt over mark og enge fra alle værelser og stuer. Alligevel er vi kun en rask løbetur eller 3 minutters kørsel fra nyt stort supermarked og 10-15 minutters kørsel fra badesøer, strande, forretningsliv og restauranter i Roskilde og Solrød. Forretningsfolk kan glæde sig ved, at Greve, Lille Skensved, Hedehusene og Ølby med talrige distributionshaller og industrianlæg ligger indenfor samme tidsrum væk. Københavns lufthavn er 31 minutter væk.

Vi tilbyder hurtigt Wi-Fi, såvel som lyse værelser med altan eller terrasse samt gratis thé og kaffe. Din egen 4G forbindelse virker også.

A terrace or balcony is featured in each of the individually decorated rooms, along with wooden floors. Roskilde Bed & Breakfast offers plenty of squaremeters for dining, working and relaxing purposes. Outside you will find a large terrace facing the sunset and our yard with garden furniture surrounded by stables from the 17th century. You will have the chance to meet and speak to people of several nationalities if you like or simply enjoy your retreat in your own mode.

Guests have access to a communal fridges on each floor as well as microwave oven, oven, toaster, coffee machine and electric kettle in the hallway. You are welcome to bring and prepare your own breakfast. Alternatively, you can order it when you make your booking.

Nice to know:

  • Terrace rooms come with private external bathrooms. Balcony rooms share bathroom facilities with maximum one more room.
  • Supermarkets in three nearby towns are five minutes away by car. A take-away 1km away also offers delivery service. Taxis can be booked in advance or will arrive within 10 minutes.
  • Three golf clubs are found within 7 km of the property. The Viking Ship Museum, the 12th-century Roskilde Cathedral as well as the medieval and charming town Køge are all within a 17-minute drive.
