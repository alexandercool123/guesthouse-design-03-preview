Guest Reviews

Køge Guesthouse hosts travellers from all over the world. Your host has travelled through 75 countries and welcomes all nationalities.

The aim is to provide the shelter your host longed for during her 800-day trip across the globe: a home away from home.

A place where you can mind your own business, or speak to other guests when you feel like it. Where you don’t need to dress for a cup of tea or coffee, but can relax and be yourself — with the chance of a chat with someone from anywhere in the world, even your own neighbourhood.
