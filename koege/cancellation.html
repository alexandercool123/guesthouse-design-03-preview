Cancellation Policy

Refundable bookings

  • If cancelled or modified up to three days before arrival, only the credit-card fees will be charged.
  • If cancelled or modified later, or in case of no-show, the total price of the reservation will be charged.
  • Cancellation up to three days before arrival is refunded in full, less credit-card fees.

Non-refundable bookings

Payment will be charged any time after booking.
