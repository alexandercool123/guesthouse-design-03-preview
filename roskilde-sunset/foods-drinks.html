Foods & Drinks

At Roskilde B&B we offer full flexibility with regards to your food preferences and breakfast habits. We leave it up to you.

You can eat out, bring food home or bring back foodstuff to prepare a light meal. If you wish to prepare your own meal, we have the facilities needed. You are welcome to use our fridges, ovens, stove, microwave oven and dine in our large living room.

2 minutes away by car, you will find a well-assorted supermarket, Rema1000 in Snoldelev, with fresh bread and other food stuff of your liking.

We have a fridge on each floor for our guests and also a large seating area downstairs, where you can enjoy a meal at your convenience. Outside you have tables on our terrace and yard.

You can make tea and coffee any time of the day for free to be consumed on our premises.

Regarding Dinner

  • Danes eat around 6pm and restaurant kitchens close rather early, maybe around 9.30-10pm

We have a take-away, Marmaris, in Snoldelev 1km away. Solrød, Roskilde and Greve offer dining opportunities 10-15 minutes away.

Practical Information

Since you have so much more common space and kitchen equipment at your convenience here than you would have found in a hotelroom, we kindly ask you to leave all common areas as you received them (clean and orderly) and to place dishes in the dishwasher or back at their place washed to ensure that it is always pleasant for everyone to enter the property. Our house assistant is only with us once a day.

Are you out for dinner and returning after 10pm we kindly ask you to please be aware not to wake up other guests, who might be asleep.
