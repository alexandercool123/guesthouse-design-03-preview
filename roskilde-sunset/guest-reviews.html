Roskilde B&B has since 2012 had guests from all over the world.

The hotelier herself has travelled through 75 countries and welcome all nationalities.

Roskilde B&B aims to provide the shelter your host always longed for during her 800 days' trip across the globe:

A home away from home...

A place, where you can mind your own business and yet, speak to other guests as and when you feel like.

A place where you do not need to dress for the occasion of having a cup of tea or coffee, but can be entirely yourself - relaxed, just as at home, and with the chance of a chat with someone from anywhere on the globe, even your own neighbourhood at times.

We are all humans...
