Foods & Drinks

At Roskilde B&B you have full flexibility with your food preferences and breakfast habits.

Eat out, bring food home or buy groceries to prepare a light meal. You are welcome to use the fridges, ovens, stove and microwave, and dine in the large living room.

There is a fridge on each floor and a large seating area downstairs. Outside there are tables on the terrace and in the courtyard. Tea and coffee are free throughout the day, to be enjoyed on the premises.

Breakfast & shopping

You are welcome to bring and prepare your own breakfast. Alternatively, order breakfast when making your booking.

Rema 1000 in Snoldelev is two minutes away by car, with fresh bread and groceries. The nearest supermarket is about 2 km away. You can also shop in Havdrup, Solrød, Roskilde and Tune.

Dinner

Danes eat around 6pm and restaurant kitchens close rather early, around 9.30–10pm. Marmaris in Snoldelev offers takeaway 1 km away. Solrød, Roskilde and Greve have dining opportunities 10–15 minutes away.

Practical information

Please leave common areas clean and orderly. Place dishes in the dishwasher, or wash them and put them back, so the space is pleasant for everyone. Our house assistant is here once a day.

If you return after 10pm, please be considerate of other guests who may be asleep.
