Rooms

Individually decorated rooms with wooden floors and a balcony or terrace.

Terrace rooms have private bathrooms outside the room. Balcony rooms share a bathroom with a maximum of one other room.

Check availability

Shared facilities

Kitchen & breakfast

Fridges on each floor, a microwave, oven, toaster, coffee machine and electric kettle. Bring your own breakfast or order it when booking.

Shared living space

Shared space for dining, working and relaxing. Complimentary tea and coffee can be enjoyed on the premises.

Terrace & courtyard

A large terrace faces the sunset. The courtyard has garden furniture and is surrounded by stables from the 17th century.
