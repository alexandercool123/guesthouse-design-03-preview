Roskilde B&B has welcomed guests from all over the world since 2012. Your host has travelled through 75 countries and welcomes all nationalities.

A place where you can mind your own business or speak to other guests when you feel like it. Relax and be yourself over a cup of tea or coffee, with the chance of a chat with someone from anywhere in the world.
